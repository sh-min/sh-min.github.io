Sunhong Min

My research focuses on robot learning and dexterous manipulation, particularly learning reusable manipulation skills from human demonstrations.

I co-authored DexStitch, a framework for learning and composing dexterous skills from human videos through contact-aware robotic inpainting.

Research

My work explores how human demonstrations can be converted into robot training data while preserving hand–object contact, and how learned skills can be composed to perform long-horizon manipulation tasks.
